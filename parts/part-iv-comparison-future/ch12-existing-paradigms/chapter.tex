\begin{definition}[Admissible Reconstruction]
A reconstruction \(x\in\stateSpace\) is admissible if it is consistent with observations in \(\observationSpace\) and satisfies all active hard constraints in \(\constraintSet\).
\end{definition}

\section{Core Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Contrast CLIO with Bayesian inference and probabilistic programming.
  \item Compare against neural, diffusion, and graph-based methods.
  \item Clarify overlap and differences with active inference frameworks.
\end{itemize}
