\chapter*{Glossary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Glossary}

\begin{description}
  \item[Admissibility] Satisfaction of all currently active hard constraints.
  \item[Constraint Propagation] Iterative local-to-global consistency enforcement.
  \item[Projection Entropy] Information loss induced by quotient or projection mappings.
  \item[Repair] Structural revision of constraints when admissible reconstruction fails.
\end{description}
